## 2. Emergency codes and numbers
- **2222**: King Salman approved a Saudi Health Council decision making 2222 the unified number for CPR emergencies in all government and private hospitals (c. Jan 2019). https://english.ajel.sa/news/king-salman-approves-several-saudi-health-council-decisions ; https://www.arabnews.pk/node/1428551
- **There is no single national MOH colour-code standard.** A survey of 24 Riyadh hospitals found 34 different codes, and only Code Red (fire) was consistent (75%). https://doaj.org/article/29b68421c28d4cc2aaa4832823d40ced ; SMJ appraisal calling for uniform codes: https://smj.org.sa/content/smj/31/12/1377.full.pdf
- Assumed set, taken from the source deck: Red fire · Orange hazmat spill · White violent person · Silver weapon/hostage · Black bomb threat · Pink infant/child abduction · Blue arrest (2222) · RRT deteriorating patient · Yellow external disaster · Grey severe weather · Brown utility failure.
- National numbers: 997 ambulance (Saudi Red Crescent), 998 Civil Defense, 999 police, 911 unified (Makkah incl. Jeddah, Riyadh, Madinah, Eastern Province). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_numbers_in_Saudi_Arabia ; https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/saudi-arabia-19-telephone-numbers-every-tourist-and-resident-must-save-1.1681300190393
- Spill thresholds have no Saudi standard. Published policies are either risk-based or use 50 mL (hazardous drugs), 1 L or 4 L. 500 mL is a defensible mid-point. https://www.ngh.on.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Code-Brown-NHNH-Final.pdf

## 3. Radiation, laser, waste
- Occupational limit **20 mSv/yr** (Saudi studies cite this as the national limit; ICRP/IAEA: averaged over 5 years, max 50 mSv in any one year). https://doaj.org/article/6f9624b9ec4b4693bbe6d7199c2288ef
- The deck's **1.25 / 1.25–3.75 / > 3.75 mSv** bands are the classic **per-quarter ALARA investigation levels I/II (125/375 mrem)**. They are review triggers, not limits. https://ehs.missouri.edu/rad/license_commitments/alara ; https://www.umaryland.edu/ehs/research-safety/radiation-safety/dosimetry/
- The deck's "2 mSv / 3 months" does not match any standard limit I found. Flag it for the RSO.
- Laser classes under IEC 60825-1:2014 (Ed. 3): 1, **1C** (new in 2014), 1M, 2, 2M, 3R, 3B, 4. "Class 3" is obsolete. https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/3587 ; https://blog.ansi.org/2018/09/laser-class-safety-1-1c-1m-2-2m-3r-3b-4/
- Waste: GCC Uniform Law for Medical Waste Management (MOH implementing regs, rev. Feb 2019). Saudi practice: yellow = infectious, red = pathological/anatomical, black = general, yellow puncture-proof sharps container. WHO adds brown for chemical/pharmaceutical waste. https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/Ministry/Rules/Documents/Uniform-Law-for-Medical-Waste-Management.pdf ; https://search.bvsalud.org/gim/resource/es/emr-74612

## 4. Labour Law (as amended, in force 19 Feb 2025)
- Sick leave (Art. 117): 30 days full pay + 60 days at 75% + 30 days unpaid per year. The deck's "25% deducted" is **correct**, and the 2025 amendments did not change it. https://noqta.tn/en/blog/saudi-sick-leave-article-117-2026
- Annual leave (Art. 109): 21 days, or 30 after 5 years. Hajj (Art. 114): 10–15 days including Eid, once, after 2 years. The deck is **correct**. https://blog.zenhr.com/en/2021/05/23/7-different-types-of-vacations-according-to-the-labor-law-in-ksa
- Widow leave (Art. 160): Muslim widow gets **at least 4 months 10 days** fully paid (≈130 days, so the deck's "130 calendar days" approximates it). Non-Muslim widow gets **15 days** fully paid. General bereavement: 5 days (spouse, parent, child), plus 3 days for a sibling (new 2025). Maternity is now 12 weeks. https://www.qoyod.com/en/learn/dismissal-protection-iddah-leave-saudi-labor-law/ ; https://www.tamimi.com/news/key-amendments-to-saudi-arabias-labour-law/
- Overtime (Art. 107): actual hourly wage + 50% of basic hourly wage; HRSD cap of 720 h/year. https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/en/article-107-saudi-labor-law/

## 5. GOSI (new Social Insurance Law, 3 July 2024)
- Legacy Saudis (hired before 3 Jul 2024): employee 9.75% (9% annuities + 0.75% SANED), employer 11.75% (9% + 0.75% + 2% occupational hazards), total 21.5%. The deck's figures equal this **legacy rate only**.
- **New entrants (first registered on/after 3 Jul 2024)**: the annuity rate rises 0.5 point each side every July until it reaches 11%. 1 Jul 2025 = 9.5%; **1 Jul 2026 = 10%**, so the current rate is employee 10.75%, employer 12.75%, total 23.5%. https://www.zoho.com/en-sa/payroll/help/employer/social-security/gosi.md ; https://noqta.tn/en/tutorials/gosi-contribution-engine-reconciliation-typescript-saudi-2026
- Non-Saudis: employer pays 2% occupational hazards. The deck's "Saudi only" is incomplete.

## 6. Health insurance / RCM
- Regulator: **Council of Health Insurance (CHI)**, formerly CCHI. On **4 March 2024**, insurer supervision moved to the **Insurance Authority (IA)**. https://ia.gov.sa/en/news/news-12.html ; https://meinsurancereview.com/News/ViewNewsLetterArticle/id/87459/type/MiddleEast/Saudi-Arabia-Insurance-Authority-now-in-charge-of-health-insurance-regulation
- Benefits update: **18 new + 10 improved benefits**, for policies issued or renewed from **1 Oct 2022**. This matches the deck's "18 added / 10 amended". Mental health was raised from SAR 15,000 to **SAR 50,000**. Dialysis rises to SAR 180,000/yr. Policy maximum is SAR 500,000. Optical glasses up to SAR 400. Kidney transplant and obesity surgery were added; the SAR 250,000 transplant limit was not confirmed publicly. https://www.zawya.com/en/life/health/saudi-18-new-benefit-added-to-health-insurance-policy-v5qo812i ; https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1588791 ; https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2115943
- The deck date (30 Aug 2023) is therefore a talk date. The policy effective date is 1 Oct 2022.
- Coding: in 2011 CCHI mandated ICD-10-AM / ACHI / AR-DRG. The SHC minimum dataset moved to ICD-10-AM 10th edition (2020). https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/ministry/mediacenter/news/pages/news-2011-5-14-001.aspx
- I found no public CHI rule for the vitamin D or HbA1c repeat intervals. Payer practice is about 12 weeks for vitamin D and 3 months for HbA1c (ADA).

## 7. Quality, patient safety, privacy
- JCI goals: 1 identify patients correctly; 2 effective communication; 3 high-alert medications; 4 safe surgery; 5 reduce HAI; 6 reduce harm from falls. Identification needs two identifiers and never the room number. https://jointcommissioninternational.org/improve/international-patient-safety-goals
- MOH sentinel event programme: a 2012 circular required hospitals to record sentinel events within 48 h. No public 24-h rule was found, so 24 h is assumed to be internal policy. https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/Ministry/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News-2012-12-29-001.aspx
- PDPL in force 14 Sep 2023, fully enforceable 14 Sep 2024, regulated by SDAIA. Health data is sensitive data. https://iapp.org/news/a/saudi-pdpl-s-first-anniversary-amendments-enforcement-and-ongoing-developments
- SCFHS: renewal is through Mumaris Plus. No public SCFHS rule on "suspension 30 days before expiry" was found, so it is treated as internal HR policy. https://lifeinsaudiarabia.net/mumaris-plus-renewal/

## 8. Figures that contradict or need correcting in the client decks
1. GOSI 9.75%/11.75% is legacy only. Saudis hired from 3 Jul 2024 now pay 10.75% (employee) and 12.75% (employer).
2. "Code Blue 2222 is national (MOH)": the number is correct, but the decision came from the **Saudi Health Council**, not MOH.
3. The radiation "2 mSv / 3 months" is not a standard limit. 1.25/3.75 mSv are quarterly ALARA investigation levels, not limits.
4. The "Class 3" laser label is obsolete (use 3R/3B). Class 1C is missing.
5. "CCHI" is outdated (now CHI), and insurer supervision is now with the IA.
6. The EBP deck date of 2023 hides the actual effective date, 1 Oct 2022.
7. The JCI claim and the CBAHI 98.67 score are not publicly verifiable.
8. Branch names "Al Andalus" and "Abhor" do not match the official site (Al Tahlia, Obhour).
9. Widow leave is "at least 4 months 10 days" in law, not a fixed "130 calendar days".
