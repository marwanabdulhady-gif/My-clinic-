## Design idea in one paragraph

The source is six slides: two activity infographics and two org charts with names and headcounts. Names and headcounts change often, and the text layer does not show reporting lines. So the module teaches **roles and routes, not people**: what Operations owns, what My Care owns, and who to go to for a patient's problem. The learner walks a patient journey, opens each activity, studies redrawn role-only org charts, sorts activities between the two teams, and then routes real-looking patient requests in a "Who do I call?" scenario. Names live in the intranet directory, linked from the last screen.
