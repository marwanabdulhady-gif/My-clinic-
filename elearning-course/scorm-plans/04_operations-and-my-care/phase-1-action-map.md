# Phase 1 — Action map & objectives (04 Operations & My Care)

## 1. Business goal

Patient requests reach the right team the first time: fewer hand-offs, faster complaint recovery, and more chronic-disease patients followed up through My Care. Measured by: complaint resolution time, internal transfers per request, My Care enrolment.

## 2. Action map

| # | On-the-job action | Why it goes wrong | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Route a patient's request to Operations or My Care | Both teams handle "inquiries and complaints" | Sorting activities; "Who do I call?" scenario |
| A2 | Know which role to escalate to inside each team | Org charts are unclear and names change | Redrawn role charts; scenario escalation node |
| A3 | Use the team abbreviations correctly | PXO, FM, DMP, CHP unknown | Flashcards |

## 3. Learning objectives

| Id | Objective | Assessed by |
|---|---|---|
| `ops-o1` | Distinguish the activities owned by Operations from those owned by My Care. | `ops-030` |
| `ops-o2` | Route common patient requests to the right team and role. | `ops-031`, `ops-032` |
| `ops-o3` | Explain the main roles and abbreviations in each structure. | `ops-033` |
