# Phase 1 — Action map & objectives (03 Welcome On Board)

## 1. Business goal

New employees feel they belong from day one, act on My Clinic's values and ethics, and handle their own HR tasks (leave, letters, licences, contracts) through the right system without repeated questions to the HR help desk. Measured by: onboarding survey scores, HR help-desk ticket volume from employees in their first 90 days, and late licence renewals.

## 2. Action map

| # | On-the-job action | Why it goes wrong | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Make everyday choices that reflect the four values | Values feel abstract | Value ↔ action matching |
| A2 | Recognise which strategy pillar your role supports | Strategy seen as "management's thing" | Pillar MCQ |
| A3 | Raise an ethical concern or conflict of interest the right way and on time | Fear, unclear route | Ethics decision scenario |
| A4 | Respect patients' rights and explain their responsibilities | Rights confused with responsibilities | Sort rights vs responsibilities; refusal-of-treatment MCQ |
| A5 | Check eligibility before requesting leave | People assume all exams or leave types qualify | Leave desk scenario; adaptive practice |
| A6 | Understand your payslip deductions and overtime | Surprise at GOSI deduction | GOSI and overtime worked examples + practice |
| A7 | Use the right system for each HR task | Emails to HR for self-service tasks | Task ↔ system matching; Oracle simulation |
| A8 | Keep your professional licence valid | Late Mumaris renewal → suspension | True/false + reminder |
| A9 | Follow ID and dress-code rules for your role | Unclear rules per role | Dress-code sort |
| A10 | Resign correctly if you leave | Notice not served | MCQ |

## 5. Pedagogy notes

- **Belonging first:** the first five minutes are welcoming and personal (icebreaker poll, purpose, values) before policy.
- **Job aid over memory:** leave and pay tables go into the downloadable *HR Quick Reference* PDF. On screen, the learner practises using the rule, not reciting it.
- **Worked example → practice** for every calculation (Sweller's worked-example effect), then one scored attempt.
- **Adaptive practice** (`hr-045`) gives extra leave questions only to people who need them, so experienced hires finish faster.
