# Phase 1 — Action map & objectives (05 RCM Part A)

## 1. Business goal

Lower the claim-rejection rate caused by documentation and coding (missing justification, non-specific diagnosis, incomplete documentation, diagnosis–treatment mismatch) and raise first-attempt pre-authorisation approvals. Measured by: monthly rejection rate by reason code, per department, 3 months before vs after rollout.

## 2. Action map

| # | On-the-job action | Why it goes wrong | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Write a complete clinical picture: CC, HPI, past history, findings, assessment, plan with duration and follow-up | Time pressure; templates copied | Sort the note sections; weak vs strong note |
| A2 | Justify every order with a clinical indication (medical necessity) | Orders feel "obvious" to the doctor | Medical-necessity MCQ |
| A3 | Code the most specific supported diagnosis, or a justified provisional diagnosis | Symptom codes are quick | Symptom → diagnosis matching; provisional diagnosis worked example |
| A4 | Avoid uncovered codes as the primary diagnosis | Codes picked from favourites | Uncovered-code sort |
| A5 | Follow work-up protocols and repeat intervals; document why when you don't | Defensive ordering; early repeats | Joint-pain scenario; timing MCQ; supplements T/F |
| A6 | Recognise why a claim was rejected | Rejections seen as "insurance's fault" | Classify rejection reasons |
| A7 | Check coverage rules for common requests | Rules change | PCOS vs menopause MCQ |

## 5. Pedagogy notes

- **Respect the expert learner**: no lecturing on medicine. Frame everything as "how the reviewer reads your note".
- **Contrasting cases** (weak vs strong note) are the main teaching device.
- **Productive failure**: the opening poll asks doctors to guess the top rejection cause; the answer is revealed on `rcma-040`.
- No real patient data. All notes are invented, reviewed by the Medical Director (B10).
