Yes. The consultation cannot be completed while a mandatory section, such as the plan of care, is empty. Mandatory-field list ⚠ Draft — pending B4.

Non-formulary

You order Medicine B, a non-formulary item (example ⚠ Draft — pending B6). The system flags it. What does that mean?

Yes. A non-formulary item needs approval first, so a formulary alternative may be quicker for the patient.

The flag does not cancel the order. It tells you the item needs approval before it can be dispensed.

The flag is about the item's formulary status, not the dose.

Ignoring it delays the patient: the item can't be dispensed until it is approved.

Why link to a diagnosis

Why must each order be linked to the assessed diagnosis?

Yes. The link explains why the order was needed. Unlinked orders are a common rejection cause (module 05).

Orders appear on the summary either way. The link is about medical necessity and a consistent record.

It is not optional in practice: unlinked orders are a common cause of claim rejections.

The diagnosis link does not show prices. It tells the reviewer why the order was needed.
