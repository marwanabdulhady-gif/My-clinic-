# Phase 1 — Action map & objectives (07 Clinical Governance)

## 1. Business goal

Fewer preventable patient-safety events and more near-miss reports, submitted on time. Measured by: identification errors, incident reports per 1,000 visits (near misses up is good), percentage of reports submitted within 24 hours, IPSG audit scores.

## 2. Action map

| # | On-the-job action | Why it goes wrong | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Identify every patient with the two approved identifiers before any care | Familiarity; same names; using room or age | Identification scenario |
| A2 | Apply the six safety goals in daily work | Goals feel like posters | Practice ↔ goal matching; 5 moments order |
| A3 | Describe how quality is measured and improved | Abstract | Structure/Process/Outcome sort; FOCUS-PDCA order |
| A4 | Score a risk and propose a response | Everyone uses different words | Matrix worked example; score; response matching |
| A5 | Classify an event correctly | Near misses dismissed as "nothing happened" | Iceberg sort |
| A6 | Report within 24 hours without fear | Fear of blame, fatigue, unclear channel | Just-culture T/F; end-of-shift scenario |

## 5. Pedagogy notes

- **Human vulnerability** fits this module: the welcome poll asks (anonymously) whether the learner has ever stayed silent about something unsafe; the mentor shares a short honest story of a near miss she reported. This makes speaking up normal.
- Every abstract model (S-P-O, PDCA, matrix, iceberg) is followed immediately by one concrete decision.
