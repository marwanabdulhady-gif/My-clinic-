## Design idea in one paragraph

The source is a 9-minute narrated video (NotebookLM-style, watermarked) with five parts: the quality standard, the six International Patient Safety Goals, risk management, the incident iceberg, and the 24-hour reporting rule. A video like this is easy to watch and hard to remember. The package **rebuilds the content natively** with new visuals and narration (no watermark, no licence question), and makes the learner do each idea once: sort Structure/Process/Outcome examples, order FOCUS-PDCA, identify the right patient when two share a name, match practices to the six goals, score a risk on the 5×5 matrix and pick a response, classify events on the iceberg, and report a near miss at the end of a tiring shift. The original video is not shipped (decision D7); an optional short "speak up" message from the Quality Director can be recorded in-house.
