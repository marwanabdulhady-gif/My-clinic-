## How to close a row

1. Write the spoken lines from the screen's own content (the Phase 2 purpose, the Phase 3 intent, the interaction's teaching point) — never the answer on a scored screen, and never a sentence about the interface.
2. Keep spoken sentences at or below 25 words and the segment within its target (±15%).
3. Rerun `tools/assemble_storyboard_docs.py <module>`, then `tools/build_motion_spec.py <module>`, then `tools/lint_planning.py <module>`.
4. Rebuild the package with `tools/build_course_spec.py` + `tools/edumints_build.py` and refresh `draft_builds_report.py` when a module's scripts change.
