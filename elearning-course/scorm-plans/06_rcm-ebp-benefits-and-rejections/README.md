## Design idea in one paragraph

The 2026 deck adds two things the script doesn't have: the **Added & Amended benefits of the new EBP** (prevention, vaccines, STDs, mental health, home care, telemedicine, diabetes programme, women's health, family planning, kidney transplant) and a **real claim-rejection log**. The package teaches the benefits as "what can I now offer and document for this patient?", with the limits that matter (sub-limits, pre-authorisation, exclusions), then uses an anonymised, rebuilt version of the rejection log as a sorting exercise and a rework scenario. An adaptive "covered or not?" practice gives extra items to learners who need them. The slide-6 log is **never** shown as a screenshot.
