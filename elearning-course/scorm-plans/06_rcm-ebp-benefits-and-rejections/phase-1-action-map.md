# Phase 1 — Action map & objectives (06 RCM Part B)

## 1. Business goal

Patients get the benefits their policy now includes, and claims for those services are approved first time; rejected claims are reworked correctly instead of resubmitted unchanged. Measured by: uptake of EBP preventive services, pre-authorisation approval rate for mental-health and home-care services, rework success rate on rejected claims.

## 2. Action map

| # | On-the-job action | Why it goes wrong | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Know which benefits are new (added) and which changed (amended) | Lists are long and change | Added/amended sort |
| A2 | Offer and document a covered preventive service, vaccine or programme | Doctors don't know it's covered | Prevention MCQ; adaptive practice |
| A3 | Respect sub-limits, exclusions and pre-authorisation | Assumed full coverage | Mental-health T/F; limits matching |
| A4 | Read a rejection comment and classify the cause | Rejections seen as random | Classify rejection log |
| A5 | Rework a rejected claim correctly | Resubmitting unchanged / duplicate billing | Rework scenario |

## 3. Learning objectives

| Id | Objective | Assessed by |
|---|---|---|
| `rcmb-o1` | Distinguish benefits added by the new EBP from benefits amended by it. | `rcmb-010` |

| `rcmb-o3` | Classify claim rejections by cause and choose the correct rework. | `rcmb-051`, `rcmb-052` |

## 5. Pedagogy notes

- Benefits are taught as patient situations, not lists.
- The rejection log is the most authentic material in the source; it becomes the core practice once anonymised.
- Adaptive practice (`rcmb-060`) lets RCM staff who already know the EBP finish quickly.
