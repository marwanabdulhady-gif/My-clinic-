## 4. Key takeaways for learners

- Operations is accountable for the **end-to-end patient journey**, KPIs for lab/radiology/pharmacy, patient satisfaction, and complaint recovery.
- **My Care** is the patient-relationship and continuity-of-care service: registration and priority bookings, follow-ups, chronic-disease coordination, referrals, and complaints.
- Every site (J1–J4, PR1, check-up unit, in-patient) has a named **leader** and a **team of coordinators or officers**.
