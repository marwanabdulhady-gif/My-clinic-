## 1. What this source is

The **HR and company induction deck** for new employees of My Clinic (عيادتي). It runs as a full-day orientation (see the agenda, slide 4). It covers:

1. **About My Clinic** — purpose, values, customer proposition, leadership, branches, history, awards
2. **Code of Ethics & Professional Conduct**
3. **Rights and responsibilities of patients and families**
4. **Human Capital / HR policies** — help desk, leave types (annual, sick, maternity, work injury, educational exams, scientific, Hajj, compassionate, official holidays), GOSI, overtime, letters, ID card, benefits, dress code, resignation, contracts, and the self-service systems (Oracle, intranet, Qiwa, SCFHS Mumaris)
5. Closing and "Thanks"

#### Leaves (slides 32–43)

**Slide 32 — Types of leaves:** Annual · Sick · Maternity · Educational examination · Compassionate (marriage, bereavement, paternity) · Scientific.

**Slide 33 — Annual leave.**
- Employees may use annual leave immediately after completing their probationary period, with prior approval from their line manager.
- **Carry-over limit: up to 5 days.** Carried-over leave must be used by the **end of March** of the following year.
- **Consulting-room physicians:** carried-over leave must be taken by the **end of June**.
- Annual leave does not include weekends, days off, public holidays, or Saturdays that fall within an approved annual-leave period.

**Slide 34 — Annual entitlement by grade (working days):**

| Grade | Days |
|---|---|
| H | 30 |
| G | 27 |
| F and Consultant | 25 |
| A to E | 22 |

**Slide 35 — Sick leave.**
- Notify your line manager if unwell. Sick leave is accepted only with a valid medical report.
- Reports from providers **other than My Clinic** need approval from the **Staff Health Clinic**; reports from My Clinic do not.
- Pay: **first 30 days — full salary; days 31–90 — 25% deducted; days 91–120 — unpaid.** (Continuous or separated periods count the same way.)

**Slide 36 — Work injury.**
- Applies to Saudi and expatriate employees; applies during work hours and while commuting to/from work.
- Report the incident to your line manager **and** HR on the same day or the following day.
- Provide documentation for treatment costs or sick leave. HR reports the case to **GOSI**. Coverage applies only to treatment invoices related to the work injury.

**Slide 37 — Maternity leave.**
- **12 weeks** of maternity leave.
- Flexible start: up to **4 weeks** before the expected delivery date.

**Slide 38 — Educational examination leave.**
- Paid leave for **academic** examinations only. Repeated exams are not covered.
- Leave days are limited to the actual exam days, not the whole exam week.
- Submit documents at least **two weeks in advance**: exam schedule, student course schedule and, after the exam, a result transcript.
- Not eligible: TOEFL, IELTS, Microsoft certifications and similar tests.

**Slide 39 — Scientific leave (annual allowance by profession, calendar days):**

| Other physicians (medical & administrative) and dentists | 5 |

**Slide 40 — Scientific leave rules.** Paid leave for academic exams (repeated exams not covered). Supporting documents need the MD's pre-approval. Scientific leave **cannot be carried over**. Days may be taken consecutively or separately.

**Slide 41 — Hajj leave.** For Muslim employees with **two full years** of continuous service. **10 to 15 days** including Eid days. An official Hajj permit copy must be submitted, otherwise not eligible. Subject to line-manager approval based on business needs.

#### GOSI (slides 44–46)
- **Slide 44** — GOSI defined as the General Organization for Social Insurance: a financially and administratively independent government entity providing social protection in KSA.
- **Slide 45 — GOSI deduction (Saudis only):** 9.75% of basic salary + housing allowance until retirement at 60. Employee deduction 9.75%; company deduction 11.75%; **total 21.5%**.
- **Slide 46 — calculation example:** (Basic 4,800 + housing 1,200) × 9.75% = **585** (deducted from total salary).

#### Letters, Overtime, SCFHS (slides 47–49)
- **Slide 47 — Letters:** requested through Oracle, with or without salary details. 
- **Slide 48 — Overtime:** pay depends on the employee's grade. The slide shows two formulas:
  - **Regular working hours:** Overtime = (Basic salary ÷ (30 days × 8 hours/day)) × **1.5** × working hours.
  - **Public holiday:** Overtime = (Basic salary ÷ (30 days × 8 hours/day)) × **2** × working hours.
- **Slide 49 — SCFHS (Saudi Commission for Health Specialties):** Mumaris is the unified portal for practitioner services. Practitioners must renew licences; HR covers the expense claim only; My Clinic does not pay penalties for late renewal. If a practitioner has only **30 days** left before expiry without renewal, HR may suspend them from work.
