## 1. What this source is

A shorter, more visual **safety-awareness deck** that complements Health & Safety 1 (file 01). It covers, in order:

1. Definition of safety, hazard and risk
2. Department safety liaisons
3. An interactive hazard-spotting question
4. Emergency codes table
5. Fire response: RACE, PASS, evacuation tools, assembly points
6. Fire extinguishers (how to use, types, agents)
7. Fire-alarm and firefighting equipment
8. Hazardous materials: definition, Code Orange, GHS, NFPA diamond
9. Chemical inventory example, safety cabinets, eyewash, spill kits
10. Hospital regulations (prohibited items)
11. Closing slide

It is best used as **the visual layer** of the course: many pages are ready-made poster-style visuals that can be reused as slide images or knowledge checks.

### Page 22 — Code Orange (spill of hazardous materials)
- Definition: *leakage or release of hazardous substances — biological or chemical spill.*
- Visual: a staff member in PPE cleaning a spill on a floor.
- Procedure text (as written):
  - *"If there is a spill of hazardous materials, we must secure the spill and remove it immediately."*
  - *"If it is a **Minor Spill**: less than 1 litre, and it is not highly toxic, reactive, or explosive, then call the cleaner supervisor with specialised trained team to clean up the spill."*
  - *"But **if the spill is more than 500 mL** or the substance is highly toxic, reactive, or explosive, then activate Code Orange."*

⚠️ **Internal inconsistency — resolve before publishing.** The slide defines a *minor* spill as "less than 1 litre" and, in the same paragraph, says a spill "more than 500 mL" activates Code Orange. A spill of 600 mL would be both "not minor" and "not less than 1 L." Health & Safety 1 (file 01, page 40) uses **500 mL or more** as the Code Orange trigger. Recommended single rule: *spill ≥ 500 mL, or any highly toxic/reactive/explosive substance → Code Orange; otherwise contact the cleaning supervisor.*

## 3. Key takeaways for the course

- **Risk formula:** Risk = Likelihood × Consequence.
- **Emergency sequence:** RACE for fire; "Code RED" call + call-point + emergency number.
- **Extinguisher:** PASS.
- **Spill:** Code Orange at ≥ 500 mL or any highly toxic/reactive/explosive substance.
- **Labels:** GHS pictograms + NFPA diamond on every chemical.
- **Prohibited items:** smoking/vaping, candles, unauthorised extensions, heaters, electrical boilers in offices, misuse of microwaves.
