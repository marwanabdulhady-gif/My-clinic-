### Slide 4 — Documentation strategy (CDI)
Six bullets:
1. **Medical necessity** — justify all services and medications.
2. **Full history** — chief complaint, HPI, past medical history, focus on clinical findings.
3. **Write specific ICD-10 codes.**
4. **Provisional or primary diagnosis** — supported by clinical data; radiology or labs will confirm or exclude it.
5. **Accurate and detailed management plan** with duration and expected outcomes.
6. **CCHI new policies awareness** — newly added benefits and amended benefits.

Plus two sub-bullets:
- **Avoid the uncovered ICD-10 codes:** obesity E66; acne L70; hair loss L66; seborrhoeic dermatitis L21; infertility N46, N97.
- **Avoid defensive medicine:** the combined supplements.
- **Avoid excessive ordering:** follow initial work-up protocols; early requests are a cause of rejection.

### Slide 9 — Added benefits with the new EBP (1): Early and exploratory examinations
**Early and exploratory examination** — insurance covers periodic examinations:
1. Mammogram (breast cancer)
2. Pap smear (cervical cancer)
3. Faecal immunochemical test (FIT) or colonoscopy per clinical need and best practice (colon cancer)
4. Behavioural or dietary consultations — healthy diet and physical activity to prevent cardiovascular disease in adults with cardiovascular risk factors, and those with BMI > 30 or ≥ 2 risk factors for heart disease
5. Diabetes screening by fasting blood sugar or HbA1c
6. Lipid profile test
7. Bone densitometry (DEXA) to detect osteoporosis

**Adult vaccination** — covers adult vaccines against common bacterial and viral infections, in line with MOH guidelines, including Tdap, PCV13, PPSV23 and influenza vaccine.

**Sexually transmitted diseases** — treatment of STDs (whatever the mode of infection), including: non-gonococcal urethritis, trichomoniasis, HIV, syphilis, human papillomavirus (genital warts), *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*, genital herpes and chancroid.

### Slide 10 — Modified benefits with the new EBP: Improving mental health
- Covers mental illness: the costs of detecting the diagnosis and treatment of psychiatric cases.
- **Included diseases:** depression; bipolar disorder; anxiety or stress; alcohol or drug use (excluding hypnosis or rehabilitation); anger management; dealing with grief or loss; schizophrenia; post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD); eating disorders.
- **Limit:** up to **50,000 SAR** for psychiatric treatment costs for psychological conditions during the policy period.

### Slide 11 — Added benefits with the new EBP (2): Home care and telemedicine; diabetes
- **Home care coverage:** more coverage for home health and telemedicine services; home-care services for inpatients (wound care after surgery, IV drugs after surgery or in medical cases, urinary catheter care).
- **Telemedicine:** coverage for telemedicine services provided by a centre licensed by the Ministry of Health, under the rules approved by the CHI.
- **Comprehensive integrated programme for diabetes care:** a complete 360° holistic programme with a personalised care plan built on the patient's health profile, delivered by a multidisciplinary team (physician, nutritionist, podiatrist, social worker, health coach, diabetes educators). Visits to eye, kidney, foot, heart and psychiatry specialists are included, plus prevention of complications.

### Slide 12 — Added benefits with the new EBP (3): Women's health and kidney transplant
- **Women's health — alternative hormone therapy for menopausal and perimenopausal conditions:** "Women's Health" benefit covers hormonal changes in middle-aged women, including alternative hormone therapy.
- **Family planning — temporary contraception:** covers hormonal contraceptives and intrauterine devices.
- **Kidney transplant:** surgical procedures covered, per the services package approved by the CHI and the limits in the policy schedule. **Kidney transplant cost: up to 250,000 SAR.**
